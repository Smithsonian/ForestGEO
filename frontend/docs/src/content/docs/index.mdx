## What the application does

- **Flexible uploads** — CSV, TSV, and TXT files with the delimiter detected for you, plus a
  guided column-mapping step when your headers don't match. ArcGIS Field Maps workbooks import
  directly.
- **Correct a census without reloading it** — a Revisions upload matches a small file of
  corrections against the rows already there and shows you exactly what would change before
  anything is written.
- **Careful editing** — edits that carry a warning or destroy data show an impact preview and ask
  you to confirm in writing; an undo appears immediately after any change.
- **Set up a new site in the app** — a four-step wizard builds the site, its first plot and
  census, and its quadrat layout, with an audit trail and retry if a step fails.
- **Automated data-quality checks** — sixteen configurable validations covering growth and
  shrinkage limits, species and attribute codes, plot boundaries, duplicate tags, and how far a
  stem has moved since the last census.
- **Nothing is silently dropped** — rows that can't be matched to a stem are kept with your
  original values intact and listed for review, rather than discarded.
- **Full audit trail** — every change is tracked, and a row keeps its identity through however
  many correction passes it takes.
- **Automatic cloud backup** — every uploaded file is preserved and downloadable later.
